# Verdict template

One verdict per draft. The flag list is what the writer reads first and acts on.

```
# Draft critique: <title>
<N> words · <N> sentences · confidence <low | medium | high> (<why: e.g. "no overrides from this writer yet">)

## Craft sheet
- sentence length: mean <m>, spread <s> <flat? yes/no>
- abstraction leaks: <none | list>
- stock phrases: <none | list>
- diction frequency: <none | word×count, ...>   (reported regardless of verdicts)
- verdict landings: <n>/<paragraphs> (rate <r>), runs of 3+: <none | locations>   (reported regardless)
- negation reveals: <none | count>
- closing suspicion: <none | the line>

## Dimensions
| dimension | pass/revise | reason (one line) |
|---|---|---|
| line aliveness | .. | every line earns its place / N could be cut |
| over-explanation | .. | examples trusted / N glosses |
| concreteness | .. | specific / N abstractions where an example belongs |
| rhythm | .. | varied / flat run at <location> |
| figurative landing | .. | figures land / N thud or mix |
| verdict landings and polish | .. | varied endings / clustered landings, frequency, or sealed close |

## Flags (the writer approves before editing)
1. [over-explain] "<line>": cut "<the gloss>"; the example already says it.
2. [abstraction] "<line>": concretize "<word>" from <source>, or ask the writer for the real example.
3. [dead-line] "<line>": cut; adds nothing.
4. [rhythm] paragraph <n>: <k> sentences of the same shape; break with a short one.
5. [figure] "<line>": sharpen or cut to plain.
6. [landing] "<line>": cut the landing; let the example end the paragraph.
7. [ending] "<line>": stop one sentence earlier.
8. [diction] "<word>" ×<n>: keep the strongest use, cut or vary the rest.
9. [taste] "<line>": a judgment call; the writer decides.

## Document tells (only for generated business documents)
- <tell>: "<quoted evidence>"

## Overall
<pass | revise, N flags>. <One sentence on the main weakness, if there is one.>
```

If nothing survives the reading, say so plainly: "Clean. No line work needed." Do not invent flags to
look thorough.
